---
title: Parameters
description: Starting values. They are guardian-set defaults, not a claim that they are calibrated.
---

These are the defaults in `ProtocolConfig`. A deployed factory may differ if the guardian changed them. Read `config()` on the vault you are using. viem returns that call as a tuple. The spam fee is index 13.

| Parameter | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `payoutCapAlphaBps` | 5000 (0.5) | Coverage cap as a fraction of locked depth |
| `holderConcentrationBps` | 100 (1%) | Max share of supply that can buy |
| `entryGuardDropBps` | 2000 (20%) | Max drop over the last hour at purchase |
| `activationDelay` | 30 minutes | Wait before the entry exists |
| Entry and claim average | 5 minutes | TWAP length |
| `persistenceK` | 3 | Samples that must still be down |
| `maxTickMove` | 9116 ticks | Clamp per new sample, about 2.5× |
| `minRecordInterval` | 30 seconds | Minimum time between samples |
| `challengePeriod` | 2 hours | Pending payout window |
| `challengeSpamFeeUsdg` | 10 USDG | Charged only when a recheck still holds |
| `protocolFeeBps` | 500 (5%) | Share of premiums |
| `minDepthUsdg` | 25,000 USDG | Locked quote required to open a market |
| `minPremiumUsdg` | 1 USDG | Absolute premium floor |
| `minPremiumBps` | 100 (1%) | Premium floor as a fraction of coverage |
| `maxPremiumBps` | 5000 (50%) | Premium ceiling as a fraction of coverage |
| `recordBountyUsdg` | set on config | Not paid inside `PriceObserver.record` today |

Severity and duration are not config fields. They are the grid in `GridLib`: 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 95 percent, and 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days.

The numbers are starting points for a testnet. They are not fitted to a history of Robinhood Chain rugs. See [Risks](/docs/reference/risks).
